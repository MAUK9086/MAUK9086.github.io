Selected for oral presentation and an APASL International Travel Award. The talk shows that the FIB-4 index misses significant liver fibrosis in 16.6% of MASLD patients it classifies as low risk, rising to 37.7% at BMI ≥ 40 kg/m². It introduces FIB-4 Plus, a supplementary model that adds BMI, waist circumference and the Hepatic Steatosis Index (AUROC 0.837 vs. 0.749 for the NAFLD Fibrosis Score).
