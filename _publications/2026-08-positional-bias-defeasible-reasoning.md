**Status:** manuscript in preparation.

In formal reasoning, a valid conclusion does not depend on the order of its premises. This work asks whether language models hold to that in nested defeasible reasoning, where a general rule is overridden by an exception, which is in turn overridden by a more specific one. It also asks whether models that reach the correct final answer get there through correct intermediate steps.

Results will be posted here once the paper is out.
